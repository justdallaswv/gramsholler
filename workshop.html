The Workshop

If you wander far enough back into the Appalachian foothills, down a quiet dirt road where the trees grow thick, you'll find our workshop. It’s a quiet little haven tucked away from the busy world, and it's where the magic happens.

When we aren't out fishing the mountain creeks or sitting around a campfire with the grandkids, this workbench is where we spend our quiet hours. It's here that raw lumber is transformed into wooden treasures built to stand the test of time.

Because the workshop is right at our family home, our doors are kept closed to the public so we can keep our corner of the ridge peaceful. But while we don't take visitors in person, we pour that same neighborly warmth and care directly into every package we wrap up and send out down the road to you.
