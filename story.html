Our Story

Hello there, I'm Gram. Welcome to our quiet little corner of the hills. Gram's Holler started out simply as a way to share the things built right here at my workbench, surrounded by family, woodsmoke, and good company.

My woodwork mostly focuses on making classic wooden toys. There is something special about creating a sturdy plaything that can stand up to years of imagination. When I am not out fishing or camping under the stars with my two amazing grandsons, you can usually find me in the shop cutting, sanding, and getting the next batch of toys ready.

With the wonderful help of my family, we pack up the truck and enjoy setting up a booth at our local fairs and festivals, like our recent trip down to Yakfest. It’s an absolute joy to see neighbors face-to-face and watch folks handle the treasures we've spent our hours making.

In addition to the wood shop, the kitchen stays busy too. I love making a variety of traditional cottage foods. We put up slow-simmered pear butter, a signature apple butter made from an honest six-apple blend, and completely homemade mini apple crumb pies. Everything here is made the honest way, built to bring a little bit of mountain warmth straight to your home.
