Our Story

Ours is green and quiet and a little hard to get to. Up here in the hills, Gram taught us that the best things in life aren't rushed. They are whittled from a block of cedar, woven thread by thread on a wooden loom, or simmered slowly on the back of the stove.

We started this shop to share a piece of the holler with the rest of the world. Every item packed in kraft paper and tied with twine is a piece of our history, made slow to last a lifetime.
