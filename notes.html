June 23, 2026

Upcoming Heritage Sale & Fresh Toys

Summer is moving right along, and the truck is nearly loaded up. We’re going to be setting up a big booth down at the local autumn heritage festival next month. If you're anywhere near the area, we'd love for you to stop by, say hello, and chat a spell in person.

Back at the workbench, those mini apple crumb pies we've been working on are finally coming out of the oven. The first few sewn toy horses are stuffed and finished up, too. We’re working on getting a bunch more children's toys prepped so the virtual shelves are full when the storefront opens down the road.

May 14, 2026

A Quick Spell Away from the Bench

The workshop doors have been closed for a few days because the grandkids came down to visit. We spent the weekend camping out on the ridge and teaching the little ones how to fish down at the old creek crossing. Caught a couple of nice ones, but mostly just enjoyed the quiet company.

Now that we're back, the kettle is hot. We're starting a massive batch of our signature apple butter, using an honest blend of six different types of apples. It's going to simmer all day long until it's perfectly thick, dark, and ready for jars.
