Gram-isms

"Old sayings are like old paths—they wouldn't be there if they didn't lead somewhere sensible." Down in the holler, these bits of folk wisdom have guided our family for generations.

"Slower than molasses in January."

Meaning:

Taking your sweet time. Out here, rushing through your work just means you'll have to do it twice.

"That dog won't hunt."

Meaning:

An idea or excuse that simply isn't going to work. Keep it honest and keep it practical.

"You can't make a silk purse out of a sow's ear."

Meaning:

You can't mask poor quality with fancy decorations. That's why we build our goods out of solid wood and honest ingredients.

"Don't get above your raisin'."

Meaning:

Never forget where you came from, and don't put on airs. Stay humble, stay kind, and remember your roots.

"Even a blind hog finds an acorn every now and then."

Meaning:

A little bit of luck happens to everyone eventually, regardless of skill. Count your blessings when it does!
