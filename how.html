How We Make It

Everything you find here is born right on the porch or at the workbench. Our woodwork starts as rough lumber cut right in our shop and sanded perfectly smooth to form generational toys, boards, and home goods. Our pantry preserves are slow-simmered in small batches, and our textiles are stitched with care using heritage styles passed down through generations.

We don't use faceless assembly lines or corporate shortcuts. While we use the right tools for the job in our workshop, it still takes time to get things just right. You can feel the maker's care and attention in every single piece.
